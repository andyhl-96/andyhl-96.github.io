Spaceballs

Spaceballs is a 3D particle physics simulator that implements Hamiltonian mechanics, allowing users to input smooth static potential functions and observe the behavior of a system of particles. Spaceballs supports features like gravitation, electromagnetism, and momentum transfer between balls.

This project was jointly developed at the Purdue Boilermake hackathon in Spring 2025 with physics student Alex Rodriguez (https://github.com/Rodri979) and myself. I mainly focused on rendering, GUI, collision checking, and debugging, while he focused on implementing the dynamics and various interactions.

Though our implementation has its issues (i.e. precision issues => issues with conservation laws), we believe that it is offers a different perspective on particle sims with the introduction of potential functions. We believe it could be a useful tool for learning, experimentation, and observation of the behavior of systems under various potentials.
