Andrew H. Liu

I am a first year PhD student in computer science at Rice University advised by Prof. Lydia Kavraki. Previously, I received my undergraduate degrees in conputer science and mathematics at Purdue University, where I worked under Prof. Zachary Kingston.

As of now, I am broadly interested in real-time task and motion planning and its applications in medical robotics. I hope to explore how we can achieve responsive planning using algorithmic or hardware enabled techniques, as well as how we can obtain acceptable safety guarantees. I would also like to see how we can most effectively marry the expertise of roboticists and medical professionals to produce the most effective care possible.

Formalities aside, I enjoy anything involving logical and strategic thinking; I am working my way upwards in chess and rock climbing. I am decent at most racket sports, particularly tennis. I like to play Elden Ring and Civilization VI in my free time, and casually do some fiction writing and game development. I hate web development.
